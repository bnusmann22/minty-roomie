Discover compatible matches, chat securely, sign a Roommate Agreement, split your rent and bills automatically, and browse a vetted catalog of student hostels — without leaving the app.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [platform.roomie](https://platform.roomie) with your university email address and complete the onboarding flow to set your budget, lifestyle preferences, and location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your student identity">
    Upload your institutional ID card. Once a Roomie admin reviews it, you earn the **Verified Student** badge — unlocking full access to the Discover directory and connection requests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Discover & connect">
    Browse the Discover directory or scroll the Feed to find roommate requests. Send a connection request to someone whose compatibility score and lifestyle tags match yours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign an agreement & move in">
    Once both parties accept, create a Roommate Agreement. Pay the one-time ₦2,000 confirmation fee to unlock the vetted hostel catalog and start splitting costs together.
  </Step>
</Steps>
