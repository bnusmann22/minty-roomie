This guide walks you through everything you need to do to go from a blank account to sending your very first roommate connection request on Roomie. The whole process takes about five minutes, and most of it is a one-time setup you will never have to repeat.

## Before You Begin

Make sure you have the following ready:

- A valid **university email address** (e.g. `yourname@unilag.edu.ng`)
- A clear photo or scan of your **student ID card**
- A rough idea of your **monthly housing budget** in naira

<Note>
  You can browse profiles and explore the app without completing verification, but you will need a verified student ID to send connection requests and access all features.
</Note>

---

## Steps to Get Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up at platform.roomie">
    Head to **platform.roomie** and click **Get Started**. Enter your university email address and create a password.

    Roomie sends a confirmation link to your inbox — click it to activate your account. Use your university email address rather than a personal one; it ties your account to your campus and makes verification faster.

    <Tip>
      If your university email lands the confirmation in spam, check your junk folder or request a new link from the sign-in page.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete the Onboarding Flow">
    Once you confirm your email, Roomie walks you through a short onboarding questionnaire. You will set up four areas of your profile:

    - **The Basics** — your name, gender, age, and a short bio.
    - **Budget** — your minimum and maximum monthly housing budget in naira.
    - **University** — your school, faculty, and current year of study.
    - **Vibe & Lifestyle** — sliders covering your sleep schedule, cleanliness standards, noise tolerance, and your stance on smoking, pets, and overnight guests.

    <Tip>
      Be honest with your lifestyle sliders. Roomie uses these answers to calculate your compatibility score with other students, so accurate responses lead to much better matches.
    </Tip>

    You can update any of these details later from your profile settings.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload Your Student ID for Verification">
    After onboarding, you are prompted to upload a photo of your student ID card. This step confirms your status as an enrolled student and earns you the **Verified Student** badge on your profile.

    Tap or click **Upload ID**, choose a clear image from your device, and submit it for review. An admin reviews your submission and approves it — this typically happens within a few hours.

    <Note>
      Verification is reviewed by the Roomie admin team, not automated software. You will receive an in-app notification as soon as your ID is approved. Your ID details are kept private and are never shown to other students.
    </Note>

    While you wait for verification, you can keep exploring the app and setting up the rest of your profile.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Browse Discover or Scroll the Feed">
    With your profile set up, you are ready to start looking for your roommate. Roomie gives you two ways to do this:

    - **Discover** — A searchable directory of students also looking for roommates. Filter by city, university, budget range, verification status, and lifestyle tags to narrow down profiles that suit you.
    - **Feed** — A social bulletin board where students post open roommate requests. You can react to posts, leave comments, and tap through to any profile that catches your eye.

    Both views display a **compatibility score** beside each profile so you can immediately see how well your lifestyle and budget align with theirs.

    <Tip>
      Sort the Discover directory by compatibility score to see your strongest matches at the top. Profiles with the Verified Student badge add an extra layer of trust to your search.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send a Connection Request">
    When you find a student you would like to room with, open their profile and tap **Connect**. Write a short message introducing yourself — let them know why you think you would be a good match.

    Your request lands in their notifications. They can view your full profile, check your compatibility score, and decide whether to accept or decline.

    While your request is pending, you can send additional requests to other students. There is no limit on how many requests you can have active at once.

    <Note>
      You will only be able to send connection requests once your student ID verification is approved. If the **Connect** button is greyed out, check your verification status in your profile settings.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Accept a Match and Sign a Roommate Agreement">
    When a student accepts your request (or you accept theirs), Roomie opens a **private chat** between the two of you. Use this space to get to know each other, discuss your preferences, and confirm you are both happy to proceed.

    Once you are ready to make it official, either roommate can propose a **Roommate Agreement** directly from the chat. This is a digital, consent-based contract that formalises your partnership inside Roomie.

    - The roommate who **accepts** the agreement pays a one-time processing fee of **₦2,000**.
    - Once both parties sign, you each earn the **Roomie Partner** badge in your shared chat.
    - Signing the agreement **unlocks the vetted housing catalog** for both of you — giving you access to verified hostel listings near your campus.

    <Warning>
      The ₦2,000 agreement fee is paid by the accepting roommate and is non-refundable. Make sure you have had a thorough conversation with your potential roommate in chat before proceeding to the agreement step.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens Next?

After your Roommate Agreement is signed, you are ready to move through the rest of the Roomie experience together:

- **Split shared costs** through the bill splitter — divide rent, deposits, and utilities in the ratio that works for both of you.
- **Browse vetted housing** in the hostel catalog and tap through to booking pages with your joint Roomie profile already attached.
- **Manage your move-out** together when the time comes, using Roomie's checkout consent flow to ensure your caution deposit is returned fairly.

To understand all of these stages in detail, read the [How Roomie Works](/how-it-works) guide.
