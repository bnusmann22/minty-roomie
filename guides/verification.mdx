Verification is Roomie's way of confirming that every person on the platform is genuinely a student. When you upload your institutional ID card and receive the **Verified Student** badge, other students immediately know your profile is authentic — and that makes them far more likely to reach out. Beyond trust, verification also unlocks the full Roomie experience, including access to the vetted hostel catalog once you've confirmed a roommate agreement.

---

## Why Verification Matters

Roomie was built specifically to tackle the scams, lifestyle clashes, and chaos that come with finding student housing in Nigeria. Verification is the foundation of that trust model:

- **Builds credibility** — the Verified Student badge signals to other users that your identity and student status have been confirmed by Roomie admins, not just self-declared.
- **Improves your visibility** — verified profiles rank higher in the Discover Directory and are more likely to appear in filtered searches when other students select "Verified only."
- **Unlocks the housing catalog** — after you confirm a roommate agreement, both you and your roommate must be verified to access vetted hostel listings near your campus.
- **Strengthens your Renter Passport** — your verified student status travels with your Roomie ID, giving housing providers instant confirmation of your enrollment without extra paperwork.

---

## What Documents Are Accepted

Roomie accepts **one document** for student verification:

- **Institutional student ID card** — the physical identity card issued by your university or polytechnic. It must show your full name, institution name, and (ideally) your year of matriculation or expiry date.

<Note>
  Upload your ID as a **JPEG**, **PNG**, or **PDF** file. The file must be under **5 MB**. Make sure the image is well-lit, in focus, and shows all four edges of the card — cropped or blurry submissions will be rejected.
</Note>

## How to Submit Your ID for Verification

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Your Profile Verification Page">
    From the bottom navigation bar, tap **Profile**, then tap **Edit Profile**. Scroll down to the **Verification** section and tap **Verify Student Status**.

    Alternatively, if you're in the onboarding flow, you'll reach the **Verify** step automatically after completing your vibe and lifestyle preferences.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the Requirements">
    Before uploading, confirm that your ID meets the following criteria:

    - Your **full name** on the ID matches the name on your Roomie profile.
    - The **institution name** is clearly visible.
    - The card is **not expired** (if your ID has an expiry date).
    - The image is clear, fully in frame, and not obscured by glare or shadows.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload Your Student ID Card">
    Tap **Upload ID Card**. Your device's file picker or camera will open. You can either:

    - **Take a photo** of your physical ID card using your device camera, or
    - **Select a file** from your gallery or documents folder.

    Once selected, a preview of your upload appears. Confirm the details are legible before proceeding.

    <Warning>
      The name on your uploaded ID must match the name on your Roomie profile exactly. If there is a mismatch, your submission will be rejected during admin review. Update your profile name first if needed.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit for Admin Review">
    Tap **Submit for Review**. Your submission enters the admin review queue immediately. You'll see your verification status update to **Pending** on your profile page.

    You don't need to stay on the page — Roomie will notify you via an in-app notification and push alert once a decision has been made.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Receive Your Verified Student Badge">
    Once a Roomie admin approves your submission, your verification status updates to **Verified** and the **Verified Student** badge appears on your profile. The badge is visible to all users who view your profile card in the Discover Directory, social feed, and chat.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Review Timeline

Admin review typically takes **24 to 48 hours** after submission. During high-demand periods (around semester resumption, for example), reviews may take up to **72 hours**. You can check your current status at any time under **Profile → Verification**.

---

## After Approval

Once you're verified, the following changes apply to your account:

- The **Verified Student** badge appears on your profile card everywhere on the platform.
- Your profile ranks higher in Discover Directory search results.
- When you confirm a paid roommate agreement with another verified student, you both unlock access to the **vetted hostel catalog** for that connection.
- Your verified status is included in your **Roomie ID** profile data, which housing providers can check if you sign in via Roomie Auth on partner platforms.

---

## If Your Submission Is Rejected or Still Pending

If your ID is rejected, Roomie will notify you with a reason. Common rejection reasons include:

- **Image quality** — the photo was blurry, too dark, or cropped incorrectly.
- **Name mismatch** — the name on the ID does not match your Roomie profile name.
- **Expired ID** — the student ID card has passed its expiry date.
- **Unsupported document** — the uploaded file was not a valid student or institutional ID.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to resubmit after a rejection">
    After a rejection, your verification status resets to **Unverified** and the upload field becomes available again. Fix the issue identified in the rejection notice — update your profile name if there was a mismatch, retake the photo if it was unclear, or request a fresh ID from your institution if the card is expired. Then follow the submission steps above from Step 1.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What to do if your submission is stuck on Pending">
    If your submission has been in **Pending** status for more than 72 hours without a decision, you can raise a support request from **Settings → Help & Support → Contact Us**. Include your registered email address and the date you submitted your ID so the team can locate your review in the queue.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use a different document if I don't have my ID card?">
    Currently, Roomie only accepts institutional student ID cards for verification. School fee receipts, admission letters, and matriculation certificates are not accepted. If you have not yet received your ID card, you can continue using Roomie with an unverified profile and submit for verification once your card is issued.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
