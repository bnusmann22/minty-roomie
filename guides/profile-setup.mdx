Your Roomie profile is the first thing potential roommates see when they discover you in the directory or come across your posts on the social feed. A complete, honest profile signals that you're serious about finding a good match — and it gives Roomie's compatibility engine the data it needs to surface the right people for you. Take a few minutes to fill in every section before you start browsing.

<Tip>
  Profiles with all sections completed appear higher in Discover Directory search results and attract significantly more connection requests than incomplete ones.
</Tip>

## Before You Begin

You'll complete most of your profile during the onboarding flow the first time you sign up. If you need to revisit any section later, navigate to **Profile → Edit Profile** from the bottom navigation bar.

---

## Profile Setup Walkthrough

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Your Basic Info">
    Start with the essentials that identify who you are on the platform.

    - **Full name** — your real name as it appears on your student ID. This builds trust with potential roommates.
    - **Username** — a unique handle (e.g. `@tolu_unilag`) that others use to find and mention you. You can change this once after account creation.
    - **Age** — enter your current age. Roomie uses this to surface age-compatible matches.
    - **Gender** — select your gender. Many students filter their search by gender, so filling this in ensures you appear in relevant results.

    <Note>
      Your username must be unique across the platform. Choose something recognisable — most students use a combination of their first name and university abbreviation.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter Your Academic Details">
    Academic information is central to how Roomie connects you with the right people. Students at the same university, in similar academic years, often share compatible schedules and routines.

    - **University** — select your institution from the dropdown list. This feeds directly into the Discover Directory filter so students at your school can find you.
    - **Year of study** — choose your current year (100 Level through Postgraduate). This helps match you with students at a similar stage of campus life.

    <Tip>
      Keeping your year of study up to date at the start of each academic session improves the accuracy of your compatibility scores.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload Your Profile Photo and Cover Photo">
    A clear profile photo makes your listing feel approachable and real. Profiles with photos receive far more engagement than those without.

    - **Profile photo** — upload a recent, well-lit photo of your face. Accepted formats: JPEG or PNG, minimum 300 × 300 px. Avoid group photos or images where your face is obscured.
    - **Cover photo** — an optional banner image displayed at the top of your profile page. Use it to show your personality — your campus, your room setup, or anything that represents your vibe.

    To upload, tap the camera icon on your profile avatar or the banner area, then select a photo from your device gallery.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Your Budget Range">
    Your budget range tells potential roommates what you can afford to contribute toward monthly rent. Roomie uses this to calculate budget overlap during compatibility scoring.

    - **Minimum budget (₦)** — the lowest monthly amount you're comfortable contributing.
    - **Maximum budget (₦)** — the upper limit of what you can realistically afford.

    Enter both values in Nigerian Naira. For example, if you can pay between ₦40,000 and ₦80,000 per month, set your minimum to `40000` and your maximum to `80000`.

    <Warning>
      Setting an unrealistically low or high budget reduces the quality of your matches. Be honest about what you can afford — you can always update this later.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Your Location or Target City">
    Tell Roomie where you're looking to live. This helps the Discover Directory and the vetted hostel catalog surface housing options and roommates relevant to your area.

    - **City** — select the city where you want to find accommodation (e.g. Lagos, Ibadan, Benin City).
    - **Proximity to university** — indicate how close to campus you'd like to live. Options range from **On campus** to **Within 5 km** and **Any distance**.

    If you're flexible, select **Any distance** and let your other preferences do the filtering.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Your Lifestyle Sliders">
    Lifestyle sliders are at the heart of Roomie's compatibility engine. They capture how you actually live day-to-day, so your matches reflect real-world compatibility rather than just shared budget.

    Adjust each slider on a scale from **1** (low) to **5** (high):

    | Slider | What it measures |
    |---|---|
    | **Cleanliness** | How tidy you keep shared spaces — 1 is relaxed, 5 is very neat |
    | **Noise tolerance** | How much ambient sound you're comfortable with — 1 is quiet-only, 5 is fine with noise |
    | **Sleep schedule** | From **Early riser** (left) to **Night owl** (right) |
    | **Smoking policy** | Whether you smoke or are comfortable living with someone who does |
    | **Pet tolerance** | Whether you own a pet or are comfortable living with one |

    <Tip>
      Be honest with your sliders rather than optimising for a "perfect" profile. A good match means someone whose real habits align with yours — not someone who looks good on paper.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write Your Bio">
    Your bio is a short, free-text description of yourself and what you're looking for in a roommate. Think of it as your pitch to potential matches.

    Keep it between **50 and 200 characters**. Good bios mention:

    - Your course or field of study
    - One or two things about your routine (e.g. "I study late but keep the common areas clean")
    - What matters most to you in a shared living situation

    **Example:** *"300-level Law student at UNILAG. Early riser, very tidy, no smoking. Looking for a calm, responsible roommate near Yaba."*
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The Verified Student Badge

Once you complete your profile, the next step to boosting your visibility is getting verified. Students with the **Verified Student** badge appear more prominently in search results and are more likely to receive connection requests from other verified profiles.

To earn the badge, upload your institutional student ID card from **Profile → Verification**. An admin reviews your submission and awards the badge upon approval.
