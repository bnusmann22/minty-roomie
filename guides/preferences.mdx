Every preference you set on Roomie feeds directly into the compatibility engine — the system that calculates how well your daily habits, budget, and living expectations align with other students. The more accurately your preferences reflect how you actually live, the better your matches will be. This guide walks through every preference category and explains what each setting controls.

---

## How Preferences Power Your Compatibility Score

When another student's profile appears in your Discover Directory, Roomie has already scored your compatibility behind the scenes. That score is calculated by comparing:

- **Budget overlap** — how closely your monthly rent ranges align.
- **Lifestyle slider similarity** — how close your cleanliness, noise, and sleep settings are to theirs.
- **Lifestyle tag overlap** — how many of your selected tags match their profile.
- **Location match** — whether you're looking to live in the same city and at a similar distance from campus.

A higher compatibility score means you and a potential roommate are more likely to live comfortably together. You can update your preferences at any time and your compatibility scores recalculate automatically.

## Preference Categories

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Budget Preferences">
    Your budget range tells Roomie what you can realistically contribute toward monthly rent. This is one of the most heavily weighted factors in compatibility scoring — roommates with overlapping budget ranges are far more likely to agree on housing options.

    **Settings to configure:**

    - **Minimum monthly rent (₦)** — the lowest amount you're willing to contribute per month. Don't set this too low unless you genuinely have flexibility; it can pull in matches who expect much cheaper accommodation than you're targeting.
    - **Maximum monthly rent (₦)** — the upper limit of what you can afford. Be realistic. Overstating your maximum leads to matches you can't sustain financially.

    **Where to update:** **Settings → Preferences → Budget**

    <Tip>
      If your budget changes mid-session (for example, after a scholarship disbursement or a change in family support), update both values straight away. Stale budget data is the most common reason for poor-quality matches.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Lifestyle Sliders">
    Lifestyle sliders capture the day-to-day habits that make or break a shared living arrangement. Each slider runs from **1** (low) to **5** (high). Roomie calculates the absolute difference between your slider values and a potential match's values — the smaller the gap, the higher the compatibility contribution from that dimension.

    | Slider | Scale | What it signals |
    |---|---|---|
    | **Cleanliness** | 1 = relaxed → 5 = very tidy | How often you clean shared spaces and what level of tidiness you expect from a roommate |
    | **Noise tolerance** | 1 = quiet only → 5 = fine with noise | How much ambient sound (music, calls, visitors) you're comfortable with at home |
    | **Sleep schedule** | Early riser ← → Night owl | Whether you sleep early and wake early, or stay up late and rise late |
    | **Smoking policy** | Non-smoker ← → Smoker | Whether you smoke indoors and whether you're comfortable living with someone who does |
    | **Pet tolerance** | No pets ← → Pet-friendly | Whether you have a pet or are comfortable sharing space with one |

    **Where to update:** **Settings → Preferences → Lifestyle Sliders**

    <Tip>
      Honesty here matters more than anywhere else. If you claim to be a 5 on cleanliness but actually leave dishes in the sink, you'll match with highly tidy roommates who become frustrated quickly. Set your sliders to reflect your realistic habits, not your aspirational ones.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Lifestyle Tags">
    Lifestyle tags are short, searchable labels that describe your living style in plain terms. They complement your sliders by making your preferences immediately readable on your profile card — and they're used as a filter in the Discover Directory.

    Select all tags that genuinely apply to you:

    | Tag | What it means |
    |---|---|
    | `quiet` | You prefer a calm, low-noise home environment |
    | `no_smoking` | You don't smoke and prefer a smoke-free space |
    | `early_riser` | You typically wake before 7 AM |
    | `night_owl` | You're usually active past midnight |
    | `pet_friendly` | You're comfortable living with animals |
    | `no_pets` | You prefer a home without animals |
    | `social` | You enjoy having friends visit occasionally |
    | `private` | You prefer a quieter, more private home atmosphere |
    | `clean_freak` | Tidiness is very important to you |
    | `relaxed_about_mess` | You're not strict about cleanliness standards |

    You can select multiple tags. Tags are displayed on your public profile and appear in search filter results when other students look for roommates with specific traits.

    **Where to update:** **Settings → Preferences → Lifestyle Tags**
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Location Preferences">
    Location preferences determine which roommate profiles and hostel listings Roomie surfaces for you. Being specific here narrows your results to students who are actually targeting the same area — which makes early conversations far more productive.

    **Settings to configure:**

    - **Target city** — the city where you plan to rent (e.g. Lagos, Ibadan, Enugu, Port Harcourt). If you're open to multiple cities, you can update this as your plans become clearer.
    - **Proximity to university** — how close to campus you want to live. Options:
      - **On campus** — within campus grounds or student halls
      - **Within 1 km** — walking distance from the main gate
      - **Within 5 km** — short commute, often served by campus transport
      - **Any distance** — no proximity requirement

    **Where to update:** **Settings → Preferences → Location**

    <Note>
      Your location preference also affects which listings appear in the vetted hostel catalog once you've confirmed a roommate agreement. Keeping it accurate ensures you see relevant housing options near your actual campus.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Move-In Timeline">
    Your move-in timeline signals to potential roommates when you need to be settled. Students whose timelines overlap are much more likely to coordinate successfully on a shared tenancy.

    - **Target move-in date** — the earliest date you'd like to move into a new place.
    - **Urgency** — choose from **Flexible**, **Within 1 month**, or **Urgent (within 2 weeks)**.

    Profiles marked as **Urgent** are highlighted in the Discover Directory to attract roommates with matching timelines.

    **Where to update:** **Settings → Preferences → Move-In Timeline**
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Updating Your Preferences

You can update any preference at any time without losing your existing matches or chat history. Changes take effect immediately and your compatibility scores with other profiles recalculate in the background.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Tap the **Settings** icon in the bottom navigation bar (or tap your profile avatar and select **Settings** from the menu).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Navigate to Preferences">
    Tap **Preferences** in the Settings menu. You'll see all preference categories listed: Budget, Lifestyle Sliders, Lifestyle Tags, Location, and Move-In Timeline.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Edit the Relevant Category">
    Tap the category you want to update. Make your changes using the sliders, toggles, or input fields provided.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save Your Changes">
    Tap **Save** at the bottom of the screen. Your profile updates instantly and the compatibility engine begins recalculating your match scores.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Review your preferences at the start of each academic session. Your budget, location, and schedule often shift between years — and keeping them current ensures Roomie surfaces the most relevant matches throughout the year.
</Tip>
