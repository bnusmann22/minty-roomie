The Discover directory is Roomie's searchable, filterable listing of students who are actively looking for a roommate. Instead of scrolling through chaotic campus WhatsApp groups, you get a clean, structured view of real profiles — each one showing compatibility details, a verified badge, and mutual connections — so you can make an informed decision before you ever send a request.

## What You See in Discover

Every profile card in Discover gives you a quick snapshot of a potential roommate at a glance:

| Card Element | What It Tells You |
|---|---|
| **Photo & Name** | The student's display photo and first name |
| **University** | Their institution and current year of study |
| **Compatibility Score** | A percentage showing how well your preferences align |
| **Verified Badge** | Confirms they have passed Roomie's student ID check |
| **Mutual Connections** | 1st or 2nd-degree connections you share (e.g. *"2 mutual connections"*) |
| **Budget Range** | Their monthly housing budget window |

<Note>
  Profiles with a **Verified Badge** have submitted and passed a student ID review by the Roomie admin team. Prioritise verified profiles — they represent real, enrolled students.
</Note>

## Available Filters

Use the filter panel to narrow the directory down to profiles that genuinely match your situation. You can filter by:

- **City** — Show only students looking in your target city (e.g. Lagos, Abuja, Ibadan)
- **University** — Limit results to students at your institution or nearby schools
- **Budget Range** — Set a minimum and maximum monthly budget to surface profiles with overlapping ranges
- **Verification Status** — Toggle to show only verified students
- **Lifestyle Tags** — Match on specific habits and preferences (e.g. `quiet`, `no_smoking`, `early_riser`, `pets_ok`)

Combining multiple filters gives you the tightest, most relevant shortlist.

## How to Browse and Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Discover tab">
    Tap **Discover** in the bottom navigation bar. The directory loads all active student profiles searching for a roommate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply your filters">
    Tap the **Filter** icon at the top of the screen. Select your city, university, budget range, and any lifestyle tags that matter to you, then tap **Apply**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse profile cards">
    Scroll through the filtered results. Each card shows the compatibility score, verified badge, and mutual connections front and centre.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a full profile">
    Tap any card to expand the full profile. Here you can read the student's bio, review all their lifestyle preferences, see their move-in timeline, and check the detailed compatibility breakdown before making a decision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a connection request">
    If the profile looks like a good fit, tap **Connect**. The other student receives a push notification and can accept or decline. See [Connecting](/guides/connecting) for the full request flow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips for Better Results

<Tip>
  Fill out your own profile completely — including lifestyle sliders, budget range, and a clear profile photo — before browsing. Roomie's compatibility scores are only as accurate as the preferences both sides have set. Honest inputs lead to better matches.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  Check mutual connections on a profile card before sending a request. A shared 1st-degree connection means someone you already have an active roommate agreement with also knows this person — a strong signal of trust and social safety.
</Tip>
