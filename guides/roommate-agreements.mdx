A Roommate Agreement is the formal commitment that turns a chat connection into a verified roommate partnership on Roomie. Once both you and your roommate co-sign and confirm the agreement, you gain access to vetted student housing options, a shared expense ledger, and your joint **Roomie Partner** badge — all tied directly to that specific partnership.

## Why Roommate Agreements Matter

Without a confirmed agreement, Roomie works purely as a discovery and communication tool. The agreement is the trust layer that unlocks the rest of the platform. It creates a tamper-evident, consent-based record that both you and your roommate have voluntarily committed to the partnership. This record is later referenced for bill splitting, checkout consent, and housing access — so every step of your co-living journey is transparent and protected.

## Before You Begin

Make sure you meet the following conditions before creating an agreement:

<Check>You have sent or received a roommate connection request and it has been **accepted** by the other party.</Check>
<Check>You have an **active private chat thread** with that roommate.</Check>
<Check>You are ready to pay the one-time ₦2,000 confirmation fee via Paystack (if you are the accepting user).</Check>

<Note>
  Both users must have completed their profile setup, including lifestyle preferences and budget range, before an agreement can be initiated.
</Note>

## Create and Co-sign an Agreement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your chat thread">
    Navigate to **Messages** and tap the private chat thread with the roommate you want to formalise a partnership with. The agreement flow lives inside this thread and is scoped to this specific connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Agreement' in the chat toolbar">
    Inside the chat thread, tap the **Agreement** button in the toolbar or action menu. This opens the agreement creation form directly in the conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the agreement details">
    Complete the required fields:
    - **Move-in date** — your planned or agreed start date
    - **Target location or campus area** — the general area you intend to rent in
    - **Budget range** — monthly rent ceiling both parties agree to

    These details are editable before the agreement is sent, but any changes after submission require both parties to re-consent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the agreement for co-signing">
    Tap **Send for Review**. Your roommate receives an in-chat notification and can read the full agreement details before responding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your roommate reviews and co-signs">
    Your roommate opens the agreement card in the chat, reviews the terms, and taps **Co-sign**. Neither party is locked in until both signatures are collected — either party can decline at this stage without any charge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay the ₦2,000 confirmation fee">
    Once both parties have co-signed, the **accepting user** (the person who received and accepted the original connection request) is prompted to pay the one-time ₦2,000 fee via Paystack. You can pay by card, bank transfer, or USSD.

    The payment screen appears directly within the app. You do not need to leave Roomie or visit any external site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agreement confirmed">
    As soon as Paystack confirms the payment, the agreement status updates to **Confirmed** in the chat thread. Both you and your roommate immediately receive your **Roomie Partner** badge and the vetted housing catalog unlocks for your partnership.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The ₦2,000 Confirmation Fee

The ₦2,000 fee is a **one-time charge per agreement**. It is paid only by the accepting user — the person who received and accepted the connection request. The initiating user does not pay.

Here is what the fee covers:

| What you get | Details |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed agreement record** | A tamper-evident co-signed agreement tied to your connection |
| **Roomie Partner badge** | Awarded to both users; visible in your private chat thread |
| **Vetted Housing Catalog access** | Geo-targeted hostel listings for your campus area |
| **Expense Splitter** | The shared bill ledger unlocks for your partnership |

<Note>
  The housing catalog is unlocked **per partnership**, not globally for your account. On mobile, you access housing through the confirmed roommate's chat header. On desktop, you are prompted to select which confirmed partnership to browse with.
</Note>

## After Confirmation: What You Unlock

Once your agreement is confirmed, you and your roommate can:

- **Browse the Vetted Housing Catalog** — explore pre-verified student hostels and housing providers near your campus, filtered to your budget range
- **Use the Expense Splitter** — create and manage shared bill splits for rent, deposits, and utilities (see [Bill Splitting](/guides/bill-splitting))
- **Display your Roomie Partner badge** — customise the badge's colour and theme directly from the chat interface to represent your joint identity
- **Share your Roomie ID with housing providers** — your joint Roomie ID lets verified housing platforms confirm your partnership without additional paperwork

## Roomie Pools: Agreements for Groups of 3 or More

If you are moving in with more than one person, Roomie supports **Roomie Pools** — group agreements that work exactly like standard two-person agreements, with a few additions.

- The agreement initiator invites all pool members from within a **group chat thread**
- Every member must individually co-sign before the agreement activates
- The confirmation fee is still paid once by the accepting user; all members receive the Roomie Partner badge upon payment
- All pool actions — bill changes, checkout requests, housing access — require explicit consent from every member before they take effect

<Warning>
  A Roomie Pool agreement does **not** activate until every single invited member has co-signed. If any member declines, the agreement returns to draft state and must be resubmitted.
</Warning>

## View Your Active Agreements

To review a confirmed agreement at any time:

1. Go to **Messages** and open the relevant chat thread
2. Tap the **Agreement** icon or the partner's name at the top of the thread
3. The agreement card shows the confirmation date, agreement ID, co-signers, and linked bill splits

You can also view all your agreements across all partnerships from **Profile → My Agreements**.
