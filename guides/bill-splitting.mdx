Roomie's bill splitter is a shared expense ledger built directly into your confirmed roommate partnership. Instead of tracking who paid what in separate notes or chat messages, every bill you create is visible to both you and your roommate in real time — with each person's share calculated automatically, consent tracked on every change, and payment status updated the moment a settlement goes through.

## What You Can Split

The bill splitter supports four types of expenses, covering every major cost of shared student housing:

| Bill Type | What It Covers | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Rent** | Monthly or periodic accommodation fees | ₦150,000/month hostel rent |
| **Deposit** | Refundable caution or security deposits | ₦50,000 caution fee |
| **Utilities** | Electricity, water, internet, generator levy | ₦8,000 monthly electricity |
| **Other** | Any other shared household expense | Cleaning supplies, shared appliances |

## Create a New Bill Split

To add a shared expense to the ledger:

1. Open the chat thread with your confirmed roommate
2. Tap **Splits** in the chat toolbar or navigate to **Splits** from the partnership dashboard
3. Tap **New Split** and fill in the following:
   - **Title** — a short label for the expense (e.g., "January Rent", "EKEDC Prepaid")
   - **Bill type** — select Rent, Deposit, Utilities, or Other
   - **Total amount** — the full amount in naira (₦)
   - **Due date** — the date by which both shares must be settled
   - **Split ratio** — defaults to 50/50; adjust if needed (see below)
4. Tap **Create Split** to post it to the shared ledger

Both you and your roommate can see the split immediately. Each person's share, due date, and current payment status appear on the split card.

<Note>
  Any change to a bill split after it has been created — including editing the amount, due date, or split ratio — requires explicit consent from both parties. The app tracks every change with a timestamped consent record, so neither roommate can unilaterally alter a settled agreement.
</Note>

## Customise the Split Ratio

By default, Roomie divides every bill 50/50. If your arrangement is different — for example, one roommate takes a larger room or earns more — you can set a custom ratio when creating or editing a split.

To change the ratio, tap **Custom Ratio** on the split creation form and drag the slider or enter percentages manually. Common examples:

- **60/40** — one roommate covers slightly more
- **70/30** — larger contribution difference (e.g., a larger room)
- **100/0** — one roommate covers the full bill (tracked for record-keeping)

The ratio is stored per split, so you can use a different ratio for rent versus utilities without affecting other bills.

## Set Up Recurring Splits

For expenses that repeat every month — like rent or electricity — you can create a **recurring split** so the bill auto-generates each month without manual entry.

1. Toggle **Recurring** when creating a new split
2. Set the **billing day** — any date from 1 to 28, representing the day of the month the split is generated
3. Confirm the recurring schedule

From that day forward, Roomie automatically posts the split to your shared ledger each month. You can pause or cancel recurring splits at any time from the **Splits** tab; changes still require consent from both parties.

## How Each Party Settles Their Share

Once a split is created, each roommate settles their individual share independently through Paystack:

1. Open the split card from the **Splits** tab or the chat thread
2. Tap **Pay My Share**
3. Complete the Paystack checkout using your preferred payment method (card, bank transfer, or USSD)
4. Your payment status updates to **Settled** automatically once Paystack confirms the transaction
5. A PDF receipt is generated and available to download immediately

See [How Payments Work](/guides/payments) for a full walkthrough of the payment flow.

## Joint Clearance for Rent

Rent splits follow a **joint clearance** rule: the overall rent status remains **Pending** until *both* roommates have settled their individual shares. This prevents a situation where one person pays and assumes the other has too.

- **Your share paid, partner's share unpaid → Status: Pending**
- **Both shares paid → Status: Cleared**

The housing provider or platform (where applicable) is notified only when the status reaches **Cleared**. This protects both parties and ensures no partial payments are silently accepted as full settlement.

## Earn the On-Time Payer Badge

Roomie rewards financial reliability with the **On-Time Payer** badge. You earn it by settling your share of **5 or more consecutive bill items within 48 hours** of each split being created.

<Tip>
  The 48-hour window starts from the moment the split is posted to the ledger — not from the due date. Paying early is always the safest way to protect your streak.
</Tip>

Once earned, the badge appears on your public profile, signalling to future roommate matches that you are a reliable payer.

## View Split History and Balances

To see all past and outstanding splits:

- Go to **Splits** from the partnership dashboard or chat thread
- Use the **Outstanding** tab to see unpaid balances for both you and your roommate
- Use the **History** tab to browse all settled splits, filter by bill type, or search by title

Each split card shows the total amount, each party's share, payment status, and the date it was settled.

## Download Receipts

A PDF receipt is generated for every settled payment. To download one:

1. Open the split card in **History**
2. Tap **View Receipt**
3. Tap **Download PDF**

Receipts include the split title, total amount, your individual share, payment timestamp, and a Paystack transaction reference.
