Roomie's Housing Catalog gives you and your confirmed roommate access to geo-targeted, verified student hostels near Nigerian universities — curated to protect you from the fake agents and rental scams that plague campus housing searches. Because the catalog is tied directly to a confirmed partnership, every listing you see is matched to your location, your university, and your shared budget range.

## What's in the Catalog

Once you unlock access, you can browse hostel listings that include:

- **Location and proximity** — distance from your campus or institution
- **Pricing** — annual rent displayed in naira, scoped to your budget range
- **Amenities** — room configurations, water supply, power backup, security, and internet availability
- **Photos** — verified property images reviewed by the Roomie admin team
- **Provider details** — the vetted housing agency or landlord behind the listing

All providers in the catalog have been reviewed and approved by Roomie administrators. You will never encounter unverified third-party listings.

## Access Requirement

The Housing Catalog is not available to all users by default. You can only unlock it after you and your roommate have completed a **confirmed Roommate Agreement** — which includes co-signing the agreement and paying the one-time ₦2,000 processing fee.

This fee is paid once by the accepting roommate and unlocks catalog access for **both partners** under that specific agreement. Housing access is scoped to your confirmed connection — it does not unlock globally for your account.

<Note>
  On mobile, navigating directly to `/housing` in your browser will show a guide prompt instead of the catalog. You must open the catalog from within your confirmed roommate's chat thread using the housing icon in the chat header.
</Note>

## How to Open the Catalog

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your roommate's chat thread">
    From your dashboard, tap the chat thread with your confirmed roommate. Look for the **Roomie Partner** badge in the conversation — this confirms an active agreement is in place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the housing icon in the chat header">
    At the top of the chat thread, tap the **housing icon** (🏠) displayed in the chat header. This icon only appears after your Roommate Agreement has been confirmed and paid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse available listings">
    The catalog opens and shows hostel listings geo-targeted to your university area. Scroll through listings, filter by price or amenity, and tap any card to view full details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Book' to proceed with a provider">
    When you find a hostel you want to explore further, tap the **Book** button on the listing. Roomie securely redirects you to the provider's booking page with your joint renter context already attached.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Desktop vs. Mobile Experience

The catalog works slightly differently depending on your device.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Mobile" icon="mobile">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Desktop" icon="desktop">

## How Booking Works

When you tap **Book** on a listing, Roomie redirects you to the housing provider's platform with the following query parameters automatically attached:

| Parameter | What it carries |
|---|---|
| `roomie_id` | Your unique joint Roomie connection ID for this partnership |
| `roommate_id` | Your confirmed roommate's username |
| `agreement_id` | The UUID of your active Roommate Agreement |

These parameters let the provider instantly recognise you as a verified student pair, pre-populate your joint renter profile, and skip redundant application forms — no re-uploading IDs or re-entering details you have already verified on Roomie.

## Why Access Is Scoped to Confirmed Pairs

Roomie scopes housing access to confirmed roommate pairs for two key reasons:

1. **Trust and safety** — Every pair browsing the catalog has gone through identity verification, compatibility screening, and a formal co-signed agreement. Providers know they are receiving pre-vetted, serious applicants.
2. **Scam prevention** — By restricting catalog access to verified pairs, Roomie ensures you only see legitimate, admin-approved listings. Unverified users cannot access provider redirect links, which closes off a common vector for rental fraud targeting Nigerian students.

If you have not yet completed a Roommate Agreement, you can initiate one from within your roommate's chat thread once both of you have accepted the connection.
