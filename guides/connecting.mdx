Connecting on Roomie is how two students go from browsing each other's profiles to having a private chat where they can discuss details, share plans, and eventually form a roommate agreement. The process is intentionally simple: you send a request, the other person decides, and — if they say yes — a dedicated chat thread opens for the two of you.

## How to Send a Connection Request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a profile you want to connect with">
    Browse the [Discover directory](/guides/discover) or scroll the [Feed](/guides/feed) until you find a student whose profile looks like a strong match. Tap their card to open their full profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review their profile">
    Check their compatibility score, lifestyle preferences, budget range, and mutual connections. Tap **View Full Profile** to see all details before committing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Connect">
    On the full profile screen, tap the **Connect** button. Your request is sent immediately and moves to a **Pending** state on your end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for a response">
    The other student receives a **push notification** and an **in-app notification** telling them you want to connect. They can open your profile and review it before deciding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request accepted — chat opens">
    If they accept, Roomie automatically opens a **private chat thread** between the two of you. You will receive a notification confirming the acceptance. From this thread, you can message, share details, and eventually progress to a roommate agreement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Accepting or Declining a Request

When someone sends you a connection request, you will see it in your **Notifications** tab and receive a push notification on your device. To respond:

1. Tap the notification or go to **Notifications → Connection Requests**.
2. Open the sender's profile to review their compatibility score, lifestyle tags, and mutual connections.
3. Tap **Accept** to open a chat thread, or **Decline** to dismiss the request without notifying the sender of the reason.

You can also view all incoming pending requests at any time by going to **Connections → Pending**.

## Pending vs. Active Connections

| Status | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Pending (Sent)** | You sent a request and are waiting for the other student to respond |
| **Pending (Received)** | Another student has requested to connect with you |
| **Active** | The request was accepted — a chat thread is open between you |

Access all your connections from **Profile → Connections** or the dedicated **Connections** section in the navigation menu.

## Connection Rate Limits

<Note>
  You can send a maximum of **5 connection requests per hour** and **20 per day**. These limits exist to keep Roomie a high-quality, intentional network — they encourage you to review profiles carefully before connecting rather than sending bulk requests. If you hit a limit, a countdown timer shows when your next request slot opens.
</Note>

## Verified vs. Unverified Users

Some actions on Roomie — including access to certain matching features and housing-related tools — are only available to **verified users**. Verified students have submitted and passed a student ID review; you can identify them by the **Verified Badge** on their profile card.

If you have not yet verified your account, go to **Profile → Verify My Identity** and upload your institutional ID card for review. Verification is free and typically processed within 24 hours.

## Managing Your Connections

- **View all connections:** Go to **Profile → Connections** for a full list of active and pending connections.
- **Open a chat:** Tap any active connection to jump directly into your shared chat thread.
- **Remove a connection:** Swipe left on a connection in the list (or tap the options menu) to remove it. This will also archive the associated chat thread.
