Roomie is the student-first roommate discovery and management platform built specifically for Nigerian university students. This page introduces what Roomie is, the real problems it was designed to solve, and the straightforward journey it takes you on — from finding a compatible roommate all the way to moving in together.

## Why Roomie Exists

Finding a roommate in Nigeria as a student has always meant posting in chaotic WhatsApp groups, scrolling through buried social media threads, or relying on word of mouth. Even when you find someone, there is no guarantee you will actually get along — and once money enters the picture, things can go wrong fast. Roomie was built to fix all of that in one place.

Here are the four problems Roomie solves for you:

- **Chaotic search forums.** Posts in campus Facebook groups and WhatsApp chats disappear within minutes. Roomie gives you a searchable Discover directory and a structured social Feed so the right people can always find you — and you can always find them.
- **Lifestyle clashes.** Moving in with a stranger who keeps opposite hours, has different cleanliness standards, or disagrees on guests can make student life miserable. Roomie's compatibility engine scores your lifestyle preferences against potential roommates upfront, so you know the fit before you commit.
- **Financial disputes.** Shared rent, security deposits, and utility bills are a common source of roommate friction. Roomie's built-in bill splitter keeps every shared expense transparent, trackable, and agreed upon by everyone involved.
- **Rental scams.** Fake agents and non-existent hostels target students every semester. Roomie counters this by verifying student identities and only surfacing accommodation from vetted, approved housing providers.

## Your Roomie Journey

Roomie walks you through every stage of the student co-living experience in a single app. The journey follows a clear path:

1. **Discover** — Search the directory or browse the Feed to find students who match your budget, university, and lifestyle.
2. **Connect** — Send a connection request. Once accepted, a private chat opens between you and your potential roommate.
3. **Agree** — Formalise your partnership with a digital Roommate Agreement — a consent-based contract that both of you sign inside the app.
4. **Split** — Divide rent, deposits, and utilities through a shared ledger that keeps both roommates accountable.
5. **Move In** — Access Roomie's vetted housing catalog, browse real hostel listings near your campus, and head straight to booking.

## Who Roomie Is For

Roomie is built for Nigerian university students who are actively looking for a roommate or planning to move into shared student accommodation. Whether you are a fresh first-year arriving on campus for the first time or a returning student who needs a new living arrangement, Roomie meets you at every stage.

You do not need any technical knowledge to use Roomie. If you have a university email address and a student ID card, you have everything you need to get started.
