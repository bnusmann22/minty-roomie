## Stage 1: Profile & Verification

Your Roomie profile is the first thing a potential roommate sees, so building it thoughtfully matters. During onboarding you set up four core areas: basic personal details, your target monthly budget in naira, your university and year of study, and a set of lifestyle sliders covering sleep schedule, cleanliness, noise tolerance, and your rules on smoking, pets, and guests.

Once your profile is live, you can upload your student ID card for review by the Roomie admin team. When your ID is approved, your profile receives a **Verified Student** badge — a visible signal to other students that you are a real, enrolled student and not a fake account. Verified profiles appear more prominently in search results and tend to receive significantly more connection requests.

<Note>
  Your student ID is reviewed privately by the Roomie admin team and is never displayed to other students on the platform.
</Note>

## Stage 2: Discover & Feed

Roomie gives you two complementary ways to find your next roommate.

**The Discover Directory** is a searchable listing of every student currently looking for a roommate on Roomie. You can filter by target city, university, budget range, verification status, and lifestyle tags to zero in on profiles that genuinely match your situation. Each profile card shows a compatibility score alongside the student's basics so you can triage results at a glance.

**The Social Feed** functions like a structured bulletin board. Students post open roommate requests with their requirements and personality, and other students can react, comment, and tap through to their full profiles. If you want to make yourself easier to find, posting on the Feed is one of the quickest ways to get inbound interest.

<Tip>
  Post on the Feed and keep your Discover profile updated at the same time. The directory catches students actively searching; the Feed catches students casually browsing.
</Tip>

## Stage 3: Compatibility Engine

Every profile you see in Discover and the Feed displays a **compatibility score** — a percentage calculated by Roomie's matching engine based on how closely your profiles align across five dimensions:

- **Budget overlap** — whether your respective monthly budget ranges overlap enough to share the same accommodation realistically.
- **Sleep schedule** — how closely your preferred wake and sleep times match, reducing the chance of disruption.
- **Cleanliness** — how your self-reported standards compare, one of the most common sources of roommate friction.
- **Location preferences** — whether you are both targeting the same city or campus area.
- **Lifestyle tags** — shared or conflicting stances on noise, smoking, guests, and pets.

The compatibility score does not make the decision for you — that is still your call. But it surfaces the information you need upfront so you can make that decision with confidence rather than guesswork.

## Stage 4: Connection & Messaging

When you spot a student you want to room with, you send them a **connection request** — a short introduction message attached to your profile. They receive a notification, review your profile and compatibility score, and choose to accept or decline.

Once they accept, Roomie opens a **private chat** between the two of you. This is your space to have real conversations: discuss expectations, ask questions, share more about your daily routines, and figure out whether you genuinely want to live together. The chat also serves as the place where Roomie delivers important system updates — like a proposed Roommate Agreement or a new bill split request — keeping everything in context.

<Note>
  You can have multiple active connection requests at once. A private chat only opens when a request is accepted, so you are not committed to anyone until that point.
</Note>

## Stage 5: Roommate Agreement

The Roommate Agreement is the moment your informal conversation becomes an official partnership inside Roomie. Either roommate can propose the agreement directly from the private chat once you are both ready.

The agreement is **fully consent-based** — it does not activate until both roommates have reviewed and digitally signed it. The roommate who accepts the agreement pays a one-time processing fee of **₦2,000** to confirm the partnership. This fee is what makes the commitment meaningful: it signals genuine intent and filters out students who are not serious.

Once both signatures are in place:

- Both roommates earn the **Roomie Partner** badge, visible inside your shared chat.
- The **vetted housing catalog** unlocks for both of you, giving you access to approved hostel listings near your campus.
- Your connection moves to **Active** status, establishing you as 1st-degree connections on Roomie's trust network.

<Warning>
  The ₦2,000 agreement fee is non-refundable. Only proceed to the agreement stage once you and your roommate are genuinely aligned on living together.
</Warning>

## Stage 6: Bill Splitting

Shared finances are where most roommate relationships run into trouble. Roomie's bill splitter gives you and your roommate a transparent, trackable shared ledger for every cost you share.

You can create a split for any shared expense — rent, security deposit, electricity, water, internet — and choose whether to divide it equally or assign a custom ratio that reflects your arrangement. Each person can upload payment receipts and mark their portion as settled. Neither roommate can quietly adjust balances without the other seeing.

For ongoing costs like monthly utilities, you can set up **recurring splits** that trigger automatically on a chosen day of the month, so neither of you has to remember to create a new entry every time.

<Tip>
  Create a split for your security deposit as soon as you move in, before any money changes hands. Having it logged in Roomie from day one makes the checkout process much smoother later.
</Tip>

## Stage 7: Vetted Housing Catalog

Once your Roommate Agreement is confirmed, you both gain access to Roomie's **vetted housing catalog** — a geo-targeted directory of student hostels and accommodation providers serving your campus area.

Every listing in the catalog has been reviewed and approved by the Roomie admin team. This is how Roomie protects you from the rental scams that are common in the Nigerian student housing market. You will only ever see real, operating providers — not fake agents or ghost listings.

When you tap through to a housing provider from Roomie, your joint profile — including your shared agreement ID and both your verified student credentials — travels with you. This means the provider already has the context they need to process a joint tenancy application, without you having to fill in the same forms twice.

<Note>
  Housing access is tied to your specific Roommate Agreement, not your account in general. If you want to browse housing, navigate to it through your confirmed roommate's chat thread on mobile, or select the relevant agreement on desktop.
</Note>

## Stage 8: Check-out Consent

When your tenancy comes to an end, Roomie ensures the move-out process is as fair and transparent as moving in. Either roommate can initiate a **checkout request** from the app by entering the planned move-out date and working through a checkout checklist — confirming that bills are settled, keys are returned, and no outstanding damages exist.

The other roommate receives a notification and reviews the request. If everything checks out, they approve it. If there are unsettled bills or damage disputes, they can reject the request with a detailed explanation, giving both parties a clear record of the issue.

Once both roommates approve the checkout:

- The housing provider receives an automatic confirmation that the joint checkout is verified.
- Any caution deposit held by the provider is released and split to each roommate's account in the exact ratio that was agreed in the bill splitter.
- Neither roommate can receive the deposit unilaterally — the release requires both approvals.

This consent-based checkout model means you never have to chase your roommate for your share of a deposit after moving out. Roomie handles the coordination so the process is clean, documented, and fair for everyone involved.
