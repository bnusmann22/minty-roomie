Badges are Roomie's way of turning your real actions — verifying your student ID, paying bills on time, staying active on the feed — into visible trust signals that other students can see at a glance. Every badge on your profile tells a potential roommate something meaningful about who you are before they even send a message.

## What badges are and why they matter

When a student lands on your profile in Discover, the first things they check are your photo, your university, and your badges. A profile with two or three earned badges signals reliability and community participation far more convincingly than a written bio alone. Badges cannot be purchased or gamed — each one is tied to a specific, verifiable action you take on the platform.

## Badge inventory

| Badge | How you earn it |
|---|---|
| **Verified Student** | Upload your student ID — a Roomie admin reviews and approves it |
| **Roomie Partner** | Complete payment for a Roommate Agreement via Paystack |
| **Good Payer** | Pay your share of 5 or more consecutive bill split items within 48 hours each |
| **Housing Verified** | A housing provider confirms your active tenancy through the admin panel |
| **Community Vibe** | Post more than 10 times on the Social Feed and receive more than 50 likes |

## Each badge in detail

### Verified Student

The Verified Student badge is the foundation of trust on Roomie. It confirms that you are an enrolled student at a Nigerian university and that your identity has been reviewed by a Roomie admin.

**How to earn it:**
1. Go to your profile and tap **Verify My Student ID**.
2. Upload a clear photo of your current institutional ID card.
3. Wait for admin review — this typically takes 1–2 business days.
4. Once approved, the badge appears on your profile automatically.

<Tip>
Make sure your ID card photo is well-lit and shows your name, photo, university name, and an expiry or issue date. Blurry or cropped submissions are the most common reason for delays.
</Tip>

---

### Roomie Partner

The Roomie Partner badge marks you as someone who has made a real, paid commitment to a roommate. It is awarded automatically the moment your Roommate Agreement payment clears via Paystack.

**What makes this badge unique:**

- It is **only visible inside your private chat thread** with that specific roommate — not on your public profile.
- You and your roommate can **customise the badge's colour, variant, and theme** directly from the chat interface, giving your connection its own joint identity.
- It signals to your chat partner that the partnership is official and the housing catalog is now unlocked for both of you.

<Tip>
Open your shared chat thread and tap the Roomie Partner badge to explore colour and theme options. Pick something that represents your dynamic as co-tenants.
</Tip>

---

### Good Payer

The Good Payer badge is Roomie's financial credibility signal. It tells every future roommate that you consistently settle your share of shared bills quickly and without chasing.

**How to earn it:**

You need to pay your portion of **5 or more consecutive bill split items** within **48 hours** of each split being created. The clock starts from the moment a bill split is logged in your shared ledger.

<Tip>
Turn on Roomie push notifications for bill splits so you never miss the 48-hour window. A single late payment resets the consecutive streak, so staying on top of notifications is key.
</Tip>

---

### Housing Verified

The Housing Verified badge comes from your housing provider, not from Roomie itself. It confirms that a vetted housing platform has checked you in as an active, paying tenant.

**How to earn it:**

Your housing provider's admin confirms your tenancy inside their Roomie-integrated admin panel. You do not need to take any action — the badge is pushed to your profile automatically once they verify you.

<Tip>
If you believe you should have this badge but don't see it, contact your hostel or housing provider and ask them to confirm your tenancy status through their Roomie admin access.
</Tip>

---

### Community Vibe

The Community Vibe badge recognises students who actively contribute to the Roomie Social Feed — posting roommate requests, sharing tips, and engaging with other students' content.

**How to earn it:**

- Post more than **10 times** on the Social Feed.
- Receive more than **50 total likes** across all your posts.

Both conditions must be met. The badge is awarded automatically once the system detects you have crossed both thresholds.

<Tip>
Quality posts that resonate with other students generate more likes than generic requests. Write a specific, detailed roommate post — mention your university, budget range, and one or two lifestyle preferences — and you will attract genuine engagement.
</Tip>

## A note on badge integrity

All badges on Roomie reflect actions you have genuinely taken. No badge can be bought, transferred, or manually assigned by a user. This keeps the trust system meaningful — when you see badges on another student's profile, you know they earned them the same way you did.
