If your Roomie account has been suspended or restricted, you have the right to appeal. Roomie's appeal process gives you a direct channel to explain your situation to the moderation team and request a review of the action taken against your account. This page explains why suspensions happen, what they affect, and exactly how to submit an appeal.

## Why accounts get suspended

Roomie monitors platform behaviour to keep the community safe and trustworthy for all students. An account may be suspended when it receives **multiple reports** from other users for behaviour such as:

- Harassment or abusive messages in chat
- Sending spam connection requests
- Posting misleading or inappropriate content on the Social Feed
- Misrepresenting identity or academic credentials
- Conduct that violates Roomie's community standards

A single report does not automatically result in suspension. The system flags accounts that accumulate a pattern of reports for admin review.

---

## What suspension means for your account

When your account is suspended, your access to key platform features is restricted or removed entirely:

- You **cannot appear in Discover** — your profile is hidden from other students' searches.
- You **cannot send or receive messages** in new or existing chat threads.
- You **cannot create or sign Roommate Agreements**.
- You **can still access the appeal page** at `/appeal` to submit your case.

Active bill splits and existing agreement records remain visible to your roommates during the suspension period.

---

## How to submit an appeal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the appeal page">
    Open Roomie and go to `/appeal` — you can access this page even while your account is suspended. You'll find a link to it on the suspension notice screen shown when you log in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the suspension reason">
    The appeal form shows you the category of community guideline that was flagged. Read it carefully before writing your appeal — addressing the specific reason directly makes your submission stronger.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your appeal statement">
    In the **Reason for Appeal** field, explain your situation clearly and honestly. Describe what happened from your perspective, provide any relevant context, and note whether you believe the reports were made in error or bad faith.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add supporting context (optional)">
    If you have any context that supports your appeal — for example, evidence of a misunderstanding, screenshots showing the nature of the exchange, or an explanation of circumstances — include it in the supporting context field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your appeal">
    Tap **Submit Appeal**. You'll receive a confirmation notification and an in-app message acknowledging that your appeal is under review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## What happens after you submit

Once you submit your appeal, a Roomie admin reviews your case. The review process typically takes **2–5 business days**.

During the review, the admin team examines:

- The reports filed against your account
- Your appeal statement and any supporting context you provided
- Your account history and any prior moderation actions

You will receive an in-app notification and email when a decision is reached.

---

## Possible outcomes

| Outcome | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Account reinstated** | The admin team found sufficient grounds to overturn the suspension. Your account is fully restored and you regain access to all platform features. |
| **Suspension confirmed** | The admin team upheld the suspension after review. The notification explains the decision. Depending on the severity, the suspension may be time-limited or permanent. |

If your account is reinstated, any Discover visibility settings, connection requests, and chat access return to the state they were in before the suspension.

## Tips for a successful appeal

<Tip>
**Be specific, not general.** Vague appeals like "I didn't do anything wrong" are harder to act on than a clear account of what happened. Describe the situation, the context, and why you believe the reports were incorrect or unfair.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Address the flagged behaviour directly.** If the suspension notice mentions a specific category — for example, messaging conduct — speak to that directly in your appeal rather than writing a general statement about your character.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Note if you believe reports were made in bad faith.** If you suspect a report came from someone retaliating against you after a dispute, say so and explain the background. The admin team considers the full context of your account activity.
</Tip>

---

## How to avoid suspension in the future

The best outcome is never needing to appeal at all. You can protect your account standing by following a few simple principles:

- **Be respectful in every interaction** — in chat, in connection requests, and on the Social Feed.
- **Avoid sending repeated or unsolicited connection requests** to students who have not responded.
- **Do not post misleading content** — fake roommate listings or misrepresented budgets and preferences erode trust and attract reports.
- **Follow community standards** on the Social Feed — keep posts relevant, genuine, and respectful of other students' circumstances.
- **Respond to disagreements through chat** rather than escalating to reports as a first move.

Roomie's community is built on mutual trust between students navigating the same stressful experience of finding housing. Treating other users the way you'd want to be treated keeps your standing strong and the platform safe for everyone.
