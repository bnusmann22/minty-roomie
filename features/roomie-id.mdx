Your Roomie ID is your permanent identity on the platform — a verified `@handle` that travels with you across every part of Roomie and, through the OAuth SSO integration, into partner housing platforms too. Think of it as your student housing passport number: it proves who you are, confirms your roommate partnership, and unlocks joint housing applications without you ever having to fill in another form.

## What Is a Roomie ID?

A Roomie ID has two layers:

- **Your personal handle** — the `@username` you choose during account setup (e.g. `@ada_unilag`). This is your individual identity on the platform. It appears on your profile card, in the Discover directory, and as a badge inside your private chat threads.
- **Your joint connection ID** — a unique identifier automatically created when you and a roommate confirm a Roommate Agreement. This ID represents the *pair*, not either person alone, and is what gets shared with housing providers when you browse the vetted hostel catalog.

<Note>
  Your personal `@handle` is created the moment you set up your account. The joint connection ID is only generated — and finalized — once a Roommate Agreement has been confirmed and the partnership payment is complete.
</Note>

## Where Your Roomie ID Appears

| Location | What You See |
|---|---|
| **Your profile page** | Your `@handle` displayed under your name |
| **Discover directory** | Handle visible on your profile card to potential roommates |
| **Private chat thread** | Roomie Partner badge (visible only to you and your confirmed roommate) |
| **Housing catalog** | Joint connection ID passed automatically to housing providers |
| **SSO sign-in** | `@handle` shown on the Roomie consent screen when authenticating with partner platforms |

## How to Find Your Roomie ID

Your personal `@handle` is always visible at the top of your **Profile** page — tap the avatar icon in the bottom navigation bar (mobile) or your name in the left sidebar (desktop) to get there.

Your joint connection ID is scoped to a specific roommate pair. To view it, navigate to the private chat thread with your confirmed roommate and look under the chat header — it will be displayed alongside your Roomie Partner badge once the agreement is active.

## The Joint Roomie ID: Built for Two

When both roommates sign a Roommate Agreement and the partnership is confirmed, Roomie generates a single joint ID that belongs to the pair. This ID is the backbone of the joint housing experience:

- It is passed as a query parameter (`roomie_id`) when you click through to a housing provider in the catalog, establishing a joint renter profile automatically.
- It links both roommates' verified identities together so that landlords and hostels can evaluate both people as a unit — not just one applicant.
- It is scoped *strictly* to this agreement. If you later form a new agreement with a different roommate, a new joint ID is created.

Housing access is always scoped to a specific confirmed agreement. On **mobile**, housing is accessible via your roommate's private chat header. On **desktop**, you'll be prompted to select which confirmed roommate connection to use before browsing.

## How Housing Providers Use Your Roomie ID

Partner housing platforms — student hostels and agencies that have integrated with Roomie — can display a **"Connect with Roomie ID"** button on their site. When you click it, you are redirected to Roomie's secure sign-in screen, where you approve what data is shared. The platform then receives your verified profile using your Roomie ID as the primary identifier.

This means you skip redundant application forms. Your university, budget range, lifestyle preferences, and verified student status are delivered automatically through the [OAuth SSO flow](/features/oauth-sso).

## Customizing Your Roomie Partner Badge

Once your Roommate Agreement is confirmed, a **Roomie Partner badge** appears inside your shared private chat thread. Only you and your confirmed roommate can see this badge — it's a visual marker of your active connection.

You can personalize the badge directly from the chat interface:

1. Open the private chat with your confirmed roommate.
2. Tap the badge icon in the chat header.
3. Choose a **color**, **variant**, and **theme** from the customization panel.
4. Changes are reflected instantly for both of you.

This is a joint customization — either roommate can initiate changes, and the result is visible to both.

## Privacy: What Gets Shared via Roomie ID

When you authenticate with a partner housing platform using your Roomie ID, only the data you explicitly approve on the consent screen is shared. The exact data depends on which **OAuth scopes** the platform requests:

| Scope | Data Shared |
|---|---|
| `profile:read` | `@handle`, display name, email, demographics |
| `verification:read` | Student verification status and verified-at timestamp |
| `network:read` | Current confirmed roommate partner details |

You can review and revoke access to any connected platform at any time from **Settings → Connected Apps**.

For a full breakdown of what each scope exposes, see the [Renter Passport](/features/renter-passport) page.
