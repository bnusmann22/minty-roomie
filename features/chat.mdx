Every confirmed roommate connection on Roomie comes with a dedicated private chat workspace — a shared space where you and your roommate can talk in real time, exchange media, receive system updates about your agreement, and access the vetted Housing Catalog. Chat is the command center of your roommate relationship on Roomie, and it is designed from the ground up for the realities of Nigerian mobile networks.

## How a Chat Thread Opens

Chat is not available immediately when you discover someone on Roomie. It opens only after a connection is formally accepted.

<Note>
  Your chat workspace with a roommate becomes available the moment they accept your connection request — not before. This ensures that every conversation on Roomie is between two people who have mutually agreed to connect.
</Note>

The flow looks like this:

1. You send a connection request from the Discover Directory or a profile card.
2. The other student accepts the request.
3. Roomie creates a private chat thread between you two and notifies both parties.
4. The thread appears in your **Chat** list, ready to use.

## The Roomie Partner Badge

Once you and your roommate co-sign a Roommate Agreement and the ₦2,000 processing fee is paid, a **Roomie Partner badge** appears inside your chat thread. This badge is visible only within your private conversation — it is not displayed on your public profile.

The badge serves as a trust signal between you two: it confirms you have an active, paid agreement and that the housing icon in the chat header is live. You and your roommate can also **customise the badge's color, variant, and theme** directly from the chat interface to give your shared connection a personal identity.

## Accessing Housing from the Chat Header

The housing icon (🏠) in your chat header is the primary entry point to the Housing Catalog on mobile. After your Roommate Agreement is confirmed:

1. Open the chat thread with your confirmed roommate.
2. Tap the housing icon at the top of the chat screen.
3. The catalog opens, pre-filtered for listings near your university and scoped to your joint agreement.

On desktop, you can also access housing by navigating to `/housing` directly and selecting which agreement to browse with.

## Offline Support and Network Caching

<Tip>
  Roomie is built as a Progressive Web App (PWA) with offline message caching. If your network drops mid-conversation, your chat messages are saved locally and sync automatically when connectivity is restored — so you never lose context or miss an update because of an unstable connection.
</Tip>

Roomie caches your recent message history so you can:

- **Read previous messages** even when you have no internet connection
- **Queue outgoing messages** while offline — they send automatically once you reconnect
- **View media thumbnails** for previously loaded images without re-downloading

This is especially useful in areas with inconsistent mobile data coverage around Nigerian campuses.

## Chat Privacy and Scope

Roomie chat is strictly **one-to-one between connection pairs**. There is no group chat feature for standard connections. Each chat thread is private and isolated — only you and your specific roommate can see the conversation.

If you are part of a **Roomie Pool** (a group of three or more roommates), each pair in the pool has its own dedicated thread alongside any pool-level coordination features.

Key privacy guarantees:

- Messages are exchanged only between you and your connection partner
- System messages are generated automatically by Roomie — no third party can post into your thread
- Chat history is preserved for the lifetime of your connection

## Real-Time Delivery

Messages on Roomie deliver in real time. When your roommate is active in the thread, you will see an animated typing indicator while they compose a reply. Delivery confirmations update as messages are sent and received, so you always know the status of your last message.
