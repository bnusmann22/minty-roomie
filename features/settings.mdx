Settings is your control centre for everything personal on Roomie. From here you can update how your profile looks to other students, adjust what notifications you receive, fine-tune your privacy controls, and manage your account itself. Keeping your settings current ensures your profile stays accurate and your Discover listings attract the right matches.

## How to access Settings

You can reach Settings two ways:

- Tap the **Settings** icon in the bottom navigation bar.
- Tap your **profile photo** in the top corner of any screen, then select **Settings** from the menu.

---

## Settings sections

<Accordion title="Profile">
  Your profile section controls everything visible on your public-facing Roomie profile card.

  - **Display name** — the name shown on your profile and in Discover listings.
  - **Username** — your unique @handle used as your Roomie ID across the platform.
  - **Bio** — a short description of yourself visible to other students on your profile page.
  - **Profile photo** — the image shown on your card in Discover, on the Social Feed, and in chat.
  - **Cover photo** — the banner image at the top of your full profile page.

  <Warning>
    Changing your username updates your Roomie ID everywhere on the platform — including any shared links to your profile. If you have already shared your profile URL with potential roommates, let them know about the change.
  </Warning>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Academic Details">
  Your academic information helps the compatibility engine and search filters match you with students at the same institution.

  - **University** — your current Nigerian university or institution.
  - **Year of study** — your current academic year (100L through to postgraduate).

  Keeping these accurate ensures you appear in filtered Discover searches for students specifically looking for roommates at your campus.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Budget & Preferences">
  These settings feed directly into Roomie's compatibility scoring. The more accurately you fill them in, the better your match quality.

  - **Budget range** — set your minimum and maximum monthly housing budget in Naira.
  - **Lifestyle preferences** — configure your sliders for cleanliness standards, noise tolerance, sleep schedule, and rules around smoking and pets.
  - **Move-in timeline** — indicate when you are looking to move in.

  <Tip>
    Students whose budget ranges overlap with yours appear higher in your Discover results. Set your range honestly rather than aspirationally to surface the most compatible matches.
  </Tip>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Notifications">
  Control which push notifications Roomie sends to your device. You can toggle each category independently.

  - **New connection requests** — when another student requests to connect with you.
  - **New messages** — when you receive a chat message.
  - **Roommate Agreement activity** — proposals, co-signs, and payment confirmations.
  - **Bill split updates** — when a new split is created or a payment is recorded in your shared ledger.
  - **Feed activity** — likes and comments on your Social Feed posts.
  - **Platform announcements** — important updates from the Roomie team.

  <Note>
    Disabling bill split notifications means you may miss the 48-hour payment window required to maintain your Good Payer badge streak. Keep this notification type on if you are working towards that badge.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Privacy">
  Your privacy settings determine who can find your profile and what they can see. See the full [Privacy and Data Controls](/features/privacy) guide for a detailed breakdown of every option.

  Key controls available here:

  - **Profile visibility** — choose between visible to all students, connections only, or hidden from Discover.
  - **Connection requests** — set who can send you requests: everyone, or verified students only.
  - **Data export** — request a downloadable copy of your Roomie data.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Account">
  Manage your account-level actions and linked services.

  - **Connected accounts** — view any third-party housing platforms you have authorised via Roomie ID (SSO).
  - **Sign out** — end your current session on this device.
  - **Delete account** — permanently remove your Roomie profile and personal data.

  <Warning>
    Account deletion is irreversible. Before deleting, make sure all active Roommate Agreements have been concluded and all shared bill splits are settled. Shared financial records tied to active agreements may be retained as required — see the [Privacy](/features/privacy) page for full details.
  </Warning>
</Accordion>

## Saving your changes

Every settings section saves independently. After making changes in any section, tap the **Save** button at the bottom of that section. A confirmation toast appears at the bottom of your screen when the update is successful.

If you navigate away from a section without saving, Roomie prompts you to confirm whether you want to discard or keep your unsaved changes.
