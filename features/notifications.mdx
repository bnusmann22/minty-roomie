Roomie sends push notifications directly to your browser so you never miss a critical update — whether a prospective roommate has sent you a connection request, a bill is coming due, or your roommate has submitted a checkout request that needs your approval. You can manage all of these alerts from the in-app Notification Center at `/notifications`, and control exactly which types you receive from your account Settings.

## Types of Notifications

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Connection Requests" icon="user-plus">
    You receive a notification whenever another student sends you a connection request on Roomie. The alert includes their name and a link to their profile so you can review their compatibility score, verification badge, and lifestyle tags before responding.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Agreement Co-Signing Requests" icon="file-signature">
    When your roommate proposes a Roommate Agreement inside your chat thread, Roomie notifies you to review and co-sign. You also receive a confirmation notification once both parties have signed and the ₦2,000 processing fee is paid — unlocking the Housing Catalog for your pair.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Bill Due Reminders" icon="receipt">
    Roomie sends reminders before shared bills linked to your active agreements are due. These reminders give you enough time to settle your portion of a split before it is marked overdue in the shared ledger.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Payment Confirmations" icon="circle-check">
    Receive a notification each time a payment linked to your account is confirmed — including your individual share of a split bill, the Roommate Agreement processing fee, and caution deposit transactions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Checkout Consent Requests" icon="door-open">
    When your roommate initiates a checkout request, you are immediately notified to review their checklist, check any outstanding bill balances, and approve or reject the request. Timely responses ensure the deposit refund is not delayed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="System Announcements" icon="bullhorn">
    Roomie occasionally sends platform-wide announcements about new features, scheduled maintenance windows, or policy updates that affect your account. These notifications cannot be disabled but are sent infrequently.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How to Enable Push Notifications

Roomie uses **Web Push Notifications** delivered through your browser — no additional app download is required. Follow these steps to turn them on.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Roomie in your browser">
    Navigate to the Roomie web app and sign in to your account. Push notifications work in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and other modern browsers that support the Web Push standard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Respond to the browser permission prompt">
    When you first sign in or visit the Notifications page, your browser displays a permission banner asking: **"Roomie wants to send you notifications."** Click **Allow** to enable push notifications for your device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm in Roomie Settings">
    Open **Settings → Notifications** and verify that push notifications are toggled on. You can also adjust which notification categories you receive from this screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your first notification">
    Roomie sends a welcome notification to confirm that push delivery is working on your device. If it arrives, you are all set.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The Notification Center

Your in-app **Notification Center** at `/notifications` stores the full history of alerts Roomie has sent you. You can access it at any time from the bell icon in the top navigation bar.

Inside the Notification Center, you can:

- View **unread notifications** highlighted at the top of the list
- Mark individual notifications as read, or **mark all as read** in one tap
- Tap any notification to navigate directly to the relevant screen — a chat thread, a bill split, a checkout request, or a profile
- Scroll through your full notification history without losing older alerts

Unread notification count is displayed as a badge on the bell icon so you can see at a glance how many updates are waiting.

## Managing Notification Preferences

You can control which notification categories you receive from **Settings → Notifications**.

| Notification Type | Can be disabled? |
|---|---|
| Connection requests | Yes |
| Agreement co-signing requests | Yes |
| Bill due reminders | Yes |
| Payment confirmations | Yes |
| Checkout consent requests | Yes |
| System announcements | No |

Changes to your preferences take effect immediately. Disabling a category means you will not receive push alerts for that type, but you can still view those events in the Notification Center.

## If Notifications Are Not Arriving

If you have enabled push notifications but are not receiving alerts, work through the following checks.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your browser's site permissions">
    Open your browser settings and navigate to **Site Settings → Notifications**. Find `roomie.app` (or your Roomie domain) in the list and confirm its status is set to **Allow**, not **Block** or **Ask**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-enable notifications in Roomie Settings">
    Go to **Settings → Notifications** inside the Roomie app, toggle push notifications off, then toggle them back on. This re-registers your browser's push subscription with Roomie's servers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your device's Do Not Disturb settings">
    On mobile browsers, system-level **Do Not Disturb** or **Focus Mode** settings can suppress incoming push notifications even when browser permissions are granted. Check your device settings and whitelist Roomie if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try a different browser or device">
    If the issue persists, open Roomie in a different browser (for example, Chrome if you were using Firefox) and re-grant notification permission. This helps isolate whether the problem is browser-specific.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact support">
    If none of the above steps resolve the issue, contact Roomie support from the **Help** section in your account menu. Include your browser name, version, and the device type so the team can diagnose the push subscription.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Roomie push notifications are browser-based (Web Push). They do not require a native iOS or Android app. However, iOS Safari has limited Web Push support on older versions — make sure your Safari browser is updated to iOS 16.4 or later for full functionality.
</Note>
