Roomie is built on trust between students — and that trust depends on giving you real control over what you share, who sees it, and how your personal data is handled. This page walks you through every privacy decision available to you, from what strangers see on your profile card to how you can export or permanently delete your data.

## What's always visible to everyone

By default, a small set of information is shown publicly to allow other students to identify you on the platform. This includes:

- Your **display name**
- Your **university**
- Your **profile photo**

<Note>
You can hide your profile photo from public view in **Settings → Privacy**. If you do, a placeholder avatar replaces it on your Discover card and profile page. Your display name and university remain visible to allow other students to recognise and trust your listing.
</Note>

---

## What's only visible to your connections

More sensitive details about your lifestyle and finances are restricted to students you are directly connected with — either through a confirmed agreement or an accepted connection request.

Information shown **only to your connections:**

- Full lifestyle preference details (cleanliness, noise tolerance, sleep schedule, smoking and pet rules)
- Your specific budget range (minimum and maximum figures)
- Access to direct chat with you

Students browsing Discover see a summary of your preferences — enough to assess compatibility — but not the granular details. Once you establish a connection, the full picture becomes visible.

## Profile visibility settings

You have three levels of profile visibility to choose from:

<Note>
Go to **Settings → Privacy → Profile Visibility** to change this setting at any time.
</Note>

| Setting | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Visible to all** | Your profile appears in Discover searches and on the Social Feed for all Roomie users |
| **Connections only** | Your profile is hidden from general Discover results; only students you are already connected to can view it |
| **Hidden** | Your profile does not appear anywhere on the platform; you can still browse Discover but no one can find you |

Switching to **Connections only** or **Hidden** is useful if you have already found a roommate and want to stop receiving new connection requests while you finalise your agreement.

## Who can send you connection requests

By default, any Roomie user can send you a connection request. You can tighten this to **Verified Students only** — meaning only users who have earned the Verified Student badge by submitting and having an admin approve their student ID.

<Note>
Limiting requests to verified students significantly reduces the chance of receiving low-quality or spam connection requests, and raises the baseline trust level of everyone who can reach you.
</Note>

To change this, go to **Settings → Privacy → Connection Requests**.

## How your data is stored and used

Roomie stores your personal information securely on servers maintained by GIGS Rentals, the data controller for the platform. Your data is used solely to power the Roomie service — matching, messaging, bill splitting, and housing discovery.

**Roomie does not sell your personal data to third parties.**

When you authorise a housing provider through Roomie ID (SSO), only the profile fields within the scope you explicitly approved are shared. You can revoke that access at any time from **Settings → Account → Connected Accounts**.

## Requesting a copy of your data

You have the right to a full export of the personal data Roomie holds about you.

To request your data:

1. Go to **Settings → Privacy → Data Export**.
2. Tap **Request Data Export**.
3. Roomie prepares your export file — this typically takes up to 48 hours.
4. You receive a notification and download link once the file is ready.

The export includes your profile information, post history, message metadata, bill split records, and badge history in a portable format.

---

## Deleting your account

Permanently deleting your account removes your profile, personal information, posts, and message history from Roomie.

To delete your account:

1. Go to **Settings → Account → Delete Account**.
2. Read the confirmation details and tap **Continue**.
3. Enter your password to confirm the request.
4. Your account enters a 14-day grace period — you can log back in within this window to cancel the deletion.
5. After 14 days, your account and associated data are permanently removed.

<Note>
**What happens to shared data:** Records that are part of a joint financial history — such as completed Roommate Agreements, bill split ledgers, and checkout consent logs — may be retained for a limited period as required for financial record-keeping and dispute resolution. These records do not identify you publicly and are only accessible to GIGS Rentals administrators. Your personal profile data is deleted immediately after the grace period ends.
</Note>
